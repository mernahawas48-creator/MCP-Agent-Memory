# Rate Exception and Discount Authority Policy

## RE-1 — Delegated Limit

An authenticated sales representative or finance manager may approve a pending
discount at or below 15 percent.

## RE-2 — Above-Authority Discount

A discount above 15 percent requires an authenticated finance manager and an
explicit human approval or rejection.

## RE-3 — Resolution

Only a pending rate exception may be resolved. Repeating a decision on an
already resolved request must not create another update.

## RE-4 — Independent Decisions

Approving a discount does not release a credit hold or change an overdue
invoice. Those actions follow separate workflows.
