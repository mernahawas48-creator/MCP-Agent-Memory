# Portfolio Credit Risk Guidelines

## PR-1 — Risk Signals

Portfolio review considers balance-to-credit-limit ratio, active credit holds,
overdue invoice age, and current credit status.

## PR-2 — Review Priority

Finance reviewers examine severe holds first, then minor holds, then accounts
with high balance exposure or materially overdue invoices.

## PR-3 — Decision Support

The portfolio sweep prioritizes review. It cannot automatically release a hold
or approve an above-authority discount.
